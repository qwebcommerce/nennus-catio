## Top defects (by severity)
1. **Paid but never confirmed, plus stock oversold (FR-36/NFR-07).** Stock availability is checked outside the hold transaction. Repro: product with 1 free unit, 6 parallel `POST /api/shop/checkout` (PICKUP, qty 1) → all 201. Send a signed StatusId=2 for each → 3 return 409 "Stock changed before payment completed". Those customers have paid, their orders stay PENDING_PAYMENT, and they later auto-cancel with no review flag or refund.
2. **Delivery seats oversold (FR-41).** Repro: window with seats=1, 6 parallel delivery checkouts for the same date → 5×201.
3. **Hold not released or committed exactly once across instances (Hold once/NFR-07).** Repro: run two server processes on the same DB. Send the same signed success webhook to both at once → stock decremented twice (two HOLD_COMMIT movements). Or POST `/api/online-orders/:id/status {status:CANCELLED}` to both at once → stock restored twice.
4. **Refunds can exceed the amount paid (FR-77).** Repro: confirmed 50 QAR order, refund 10, then 3 parallel `POST /api/online-orders/:id/refund {amount:30}` → all 201, 100 QAR refunded through the gateway.
5. **Late payments are not held for staff (FR-79).** Repro: checkout, let the hold expire. (a) Before any sweep, a signed StatusId=2 → order CONFIRMED automatically. (b) After a sweep (any GET /api/shop/meta), the same webhook is ignored, the order stays CANCELLED with paymentReview=false, and the money is never surfaced. The wrong-amount review is also lost once the hold expires (/resolve-payment → 404).
6. **BOGO gives everything free (FR-73/NFR-08).** Repro: promotion `{kind:"BOGO", productId:P, buyQty:1, getQty:1, code:"X"}`, checkout P×2 with code X → both units discounted. With another product offer on P the order total becomes 0.00.
7. **A cancelled paid order cannot be refunded (FR-38/77).** The customer cancels at CONFIRMED (amountPaid 50, webStatus CANCELLED), then the refund endpoint returns 400 "no confirmed card payment".
8. **Delivery fee and date can be dodged (FR-34/40, NFR-08).** A delivery checkout without `areaId` → fee 0. A past `deliveryDate` is accepted.
9. **Staff cannot cancel once an order is OUT_FOR_DELIVERY or READY_FOR_PICKUP (FR-39).** Delivery setup has no edit or deactivate and no input validation (FR-40).
10. **/api/shop/meta seatsLeft ignores the date** (shop.js:163-177). A lesser issue: web orders have no short reference (NFR-22).
