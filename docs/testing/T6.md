## Top defects (by severity)

1. **Money model breaks NFR-03 and gives wrong totals** (NFR-03, FR-110, FR-111). Amounts are numeric riyals and JS float sums are used. Repro: sell a product priced 0.10 and one priced 0.20 (CARD), then `GET /api/finance/summary?from=<t0>&to=<t1>` returns `revenue: 0.30000000000000004`. `POST /api/expenses {amount:1.005}` stores 1.00. Product price 10.005 is stored as-is.
2. **P&L puts refunds in the wrong period** (FR-110, FR-111). Repro: sale on day A, refund on day B. `finance/summary` for day B shows refunds 0; day A shows the refund. The cause is filtering on `sale.createdAt` (finance.js:25-46, exports.js:166-175).
3. **Finance end date is exclusive, so single-day and inclusive ranges drop the last day** (FR-110, FR-111, FR-112). Repro: `GET /api/finance/summary?from=2026-11-16&to=2026-11-16` returns all zeros on a day with sales, and the UI sends exactly that. Day boundaries are UTC, not Qatar time.
4. **Email has no queue or retry, and a failed send breaks the business request** (NFR-17, FR-85, FR-86). Repro: run the server with an unreachable Resend and call `POST /api/shop/register`. It returns 500, but the customer row exists and no email is queued. The checkout low-stock alert would make a committed sale return 500 in the same way.
5. **Expenses cannot record cash vs bank** (FR-108, and FR-111 money out). The Expense schema has no such field and `paidFrom` is dropped.
6. **PDF exports are unusable and there is no Arabic export** (FR-82, FR-112). All rows go on one off-page text line and non-ASCII is stripped. `lang=ar` leaves headers and values in English. Repro: `GET /api/exports/bills?format=pdf` and render it; only the first row is visible.
7. **Dashboard "today's grooming" counts future bookings** (FR-81). dashboard.js:100-107 has no upper bound. Live it showed 1 for a 2026-11-18 appointment.
8. **Customer emails are not in the customer's language** (FR-85). Bodies are English templates, and verification and reset are English only (mailer.js, shop.js:292-307 and 401).
9. **No finance list of sales and refunds by channel or type, and no export of one** (FR-103). The sales API ignores channel filters and the refunds API has no date filter.
10. **Supplier bills cannot be listed by supplier or age** (FR-104). The UI only does full BANK payment (FR-105). Unknown payment methods are coerced to CASH, and 0.004 creates a 0.00 payment.
11. **No stock-value export** (FR-112), **no daily backup schedule** (NFR-19), and **the owner cannot change the business name** (FR-83).
12. **The automations API offers SMS and WhatsApp channels and logs fake "SENT" rows** (FR-87).
